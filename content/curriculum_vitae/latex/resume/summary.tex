\begin{cvparagraph}

I'm a recent M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence graduate from the University of Porto, looking for an opportunity in machine learning. My thesis extended the NegotiationArena framework to open-weight language models, evaluating inference-time techniques such as Self-Refine and multi-agent deliberation for improving agent negotiation performance. As a student researcher, I studied how class imbalance affects synthetic tabular data generation, training and comparing deep generative models including DDPM, CTGAN, and TVAE.

Alongside my studies, I've spent the past few years volunteering across several student associations. I was part of the small team that redesigned UNI, the open-source mobile app used across the University of Porto, and more recently served as IT Manager at ESN Porto, supporting the 3500+ exchange students who pass through the city each semester. 

\end{cvparagraph}
